Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Cinevra is a movie and TV discovery website operated by Rafif Shula Syandana. This policy explains what information we use when you browse, sign in, save your collection, or share something with the Community.

Information we useWhat is sharedYour choicesContact

Information we use

You can browse Cinevra without an account. Guest watchlists, custom lists, ratings, Diary notes, viewing dates, watched episodes, recently viewed titles, and preferences can be stored in your browser.

If you create an account with email and password, Supabase Auth processes your email and password to authenticate you. Cinevra does not display or store your password in your movie collection.

If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Supabase process the sign-in. We receive basic account information, which may include your Google account identifier, email address, name, and profile picture. We use this information to identify and authenticate your account. Cinevra does not request access to Gmail messages, contacts, or Google Drive files.

When you are signed in, your watchlist, watched status, custom lists, ratings, Diary notes, viewing dates, title and poster snapshots, and watched episode progress are stored with your account in Supabase.

How we use your information

We use account information to sign you in and connect you to your collection. Saved collection data powers your library, Diary, episode tracking, personal statistics, and recommendations, and lets you access your collection on another device.

Guest data is imported into your account only when you choose to import it. The browser copy is not automatically deleted after import. Recently viewed titles and display preferences remain local to your browser.

We also use information you submit to handle Community activity, reports, support requests, and requests about your data. We do not sell your personal information or use Google account data for advertising.

Private collections and public sharing

Your account collection is not published automatically. Database access rules limit normal application access to your own private collection. The operator and service providers may access stored information when necessary to operate the service, address abuse, or handle your request.

Community publication is optional. If you publish a review or list, visitors can see the public name, text, selected titles and posters, optional rating, spoiler indicator, publication date, and reaction count. Your email, private Diary notes, viewing dates, and episode progress are not automatically included in that public snapshot.

Likes and reports are associated with your account to support those features and moderation. Other visitors see the reaction count, not a list of people who reacted. Reports are available to the operator for review.

You can edit or unpublish your Community posts. Unpublishing removes a post from the public feed but does not necessarily erase the stored record. Copies of a public list already saved by someone else remain in their collection.

Shared-list links contain a snapshot of the list. Anyone with the link can read it, and changes to your original list do not revoke copies or previously shared links. Avoid including private information in public posts or shared-list names and descriptions.

Services used by Cinevra

  • Supabase provides authentication and account data storage. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google provides optional Google sign-in. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • TMDB provides movie data and images. Your browser sends catalog requests, such as searches, title IDs, and filters, directly to TMDB. Cinevra does not send your private Diary text or account email in those requests. See the TMDB Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel hosts the website and may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser details, and request logs. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube and Google Fonts may receive technical connection information when you play an embedded trailer or load the site's fonts. Their use is covered by Google's privacy policy.

These providers may process information in countries outside your own. External streaming links take you to services governed by their own policies.

Browser storage and sessions

Cinevra uses local storage for guest collections and preferences, session storage for temporary movie-data caching, and browser storage managed by Supabase to maintain authentication. Clearing site data can remove your guest collection and sign you out. Signing out does not delete your account collection or the guest data left in that browser.

On a shared device, sign out when finished. If you clear browser storage, export any guest collection you want to keep first.

Retention and security

Guest data remains in your browser until you remove it, clear site data, or your browser removes it. Account data remains stored while you use the service unless you remove entries or request deletion. We do not currently apply an automatic inactivity deletion schedule.

We use HTTPS, Supabase authentication, and database access controls to protect account data. No online service can guarantee complete security. Provider logs, backups, and records needed for security or legal purposes may remain for their applicable retention periods after data is removed from the active service.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can browse as a guest, edit or remove saved titles and reviews, update your lists, clear episode progress, and unpublish Community posts using the website's controls. My Space provides collection export tools.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your account and associated data, email shaulaabyte@gmail.com, preferably from your account email. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before acting. Account deletion is currently handled by the operator rather than an in-app deletion button. Never send your password or sign-in tokens.

You can also remove Cinevra's access in your Google Account connections. Revoking Google access does not by itself delete information already stored in Cinevra.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions, contact Rafif Shula Syandana, the operator of Cinevra, at shaulaabyte@gmail.com.

We may update this policy as Cinevra changes. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.
